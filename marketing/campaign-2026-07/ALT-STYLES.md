# AppealMate Alternative Styles

This pack adds three deliberately different directions while keeping the claims conservative.

| Rank | Direction | Best use | Why test it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real-life relief | Paid feeds and Stories | Human context makes the service easier to understand and trust. |
| 2 | Tap to build | Reels and carousel | Simulated choices show how the guided experience works. |
| 3 | Paper trail | Organic posts and challenger ads | Editorial contrast creates a distinctive, scroll-stopping look. |
